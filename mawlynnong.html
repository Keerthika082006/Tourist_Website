Mawlynnong Village

Asia's Cleanest Village – Nature, Culture & Serenity

Nestled in the East Khasi Hills of Meghalaya, Mawlynnong is celebrated as Asia's cleanest village. Known for its eco-friendly lifestyle and warm hospitality, visitors can explore lush green landscapes, traditional Khasi homes, and experience the harmony of culture and nature.

Highlights of Mawlynnong

Living Root Bridges: Walk across bridges made from centuries-old tree roots intertwined over streams, a unique engineering marvel.

Skywalk Observation Deck: Enjoy panoramic views of the village and surrounding hills from the elevated platform.

Village Life: Explore the clean streets, traditional huts, and interact with the friendly local community.

Eco-Tourism & Nature Trails: Trek through scenic paths, discover waterfalls, bamboo bridges, and immerse in the lush greenery.
