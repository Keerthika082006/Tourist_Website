About Red Fort

Red Fort, constructed by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in the 17th century, is an iconic symbol of India’s rich history and architecture. Built with massive red sandstone walls, it served as the main residence of the Mughal emperors for nearly 200 years.

The fort is famous for its intricate carvings, massive gates, and impressive courtyards. Every year on India’s Independence Day, the Prime Minister hoists the national flag here, making it a site of great national importance.
