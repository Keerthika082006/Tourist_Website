Hawa Mahal

The Palace of Winds — Jaipur

Overview

Hawa Mahal, the “Palace of Winds”, is an iconic structure located in Jaipur. Built in 1799, it features 953 windows designed for royal women to view festivities without being seen. Its unique honeycomb architecture and pink sandstone exterior make it one of India's most photographed monuments.

Highlights

  • 953 Jharokha windows
  • Beautiful sunrise photography location
  • Symbol of Jaipur’s royal architecture
  • Located in the vibrant old city
