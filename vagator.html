Vagator Beach

About Vagator

Vagator Beach is known for its bright red cliffs, sweeping ocean views, peaceful vibe and artistic cafés. Unlike the crowded party zones, Vagator offers a perfect mix of nature, calmness and culture.

The clifftop sunset view point is one of the best in all of Goa, attracting travellers, photographers and adventure seekers every evening.
