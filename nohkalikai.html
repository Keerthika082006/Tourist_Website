Nohkalikai Falls

Majestic waterfalls amidst lush Khasi hills — adventure, photography & nature

About Nohkalikai Falls

Nohkalikai Falls is the tallest plunge waterfall in India, located near Cherrapunjee, Meghalaya. The water drops from a height of 340 meters amidst lush green cliffs.

During monsoon, the falls are full of cascading water, surrounded by mist and rainbows. Ideal for trekking, photography, and nature enthusiasts.

Local legends say the waterfall is named after a tragic tale, adding a mysterious charm to the natural beauty.

Things to Do

Trekking: Trails through forested paths with cliff viewpoints.

Photography: Capture rainbows, mist, and panoramic views.

Relax: Peaceful nature walks along the river at the base.
