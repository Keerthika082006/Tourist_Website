Baga Beach

Baga Beach is one of the most famous beaches in Goa, known for its nightlife, beach shacks, seafood, and thrilling water sports. It's a vibrant place filled with energy, music, and adventure.

Nightlife

Baga is famous for Tito’s Lane – packed with clubs, bars, and live music. You can experience fire shows, karaoke nights, EDM parties, and beach bars that stay alive until sunrise.

Water Sports

Jet ski, parasailing, banana ride, bumper ride, kayaking – Baga offers thrilling adventures for everyone looking for fun on the sea.

Nearby Spots

  • Calangute Market
  • Anjuna Flea Market
  • Chapora Fort
  • Candolim Beach
