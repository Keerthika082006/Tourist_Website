Palolem Beach — Serene & Peaceful

Palolem Beach, located in South Goa, is famous for its calm waters, crescent-shaped shore, and laid-back atmosphere. It's perfect for relaxing, yoga sessions, and kayaking along the coast. Unlike Baga, this beach is tranquil and ideal for unwinding.

Activities

  • Kayaking and paddleboarding
  • Beach yoga sessions
  • Sunset walks
  • Photography & nature walks

Nearby Attractions

  • Butterfly Beach (accessible by boat)
  • Agonda Beach
  • Monkey Island tours

Food & Stay

Enjoy fresh seafood at beach shacks or stay in charming huts and boutique guesthouses. Palolem is all about comfort and scenic beauty.
