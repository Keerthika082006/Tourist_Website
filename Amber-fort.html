Amber Fort, Jaipur

The Royal Hilltop Fortress of Rajputana Glory

About Amber Fort

Amber Fort, also known as Amer Fort, is one of the most magnificent palaces in Rajasthan. Built in 1592 by Raja Man Singh I, the fort is renowned for its artistic blend of Hindu and Mughal architecture. With its golden sandstone walls, mirror palace halls, sprawling courtyards, and majestic gates, the fort stands as a symbol of Jaipur’s royal legacy.

The fort overlooks Maota Lake and features attractions such as the Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace), Diwan-e-Aam, Diwan-e-Khas, Shila Devi temple, and intricately carved frescoes. Its evening light-and-sound show is a stunning narration of its glorious past.
