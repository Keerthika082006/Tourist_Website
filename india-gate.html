India Gate

The Iconic War Memorial of Delhi

About India Gate

India Gate is a 42-meter tall war memorial located in the heart of New Delhi, built in memory of the Indian soldiers who sacrificed their lives during World War I. It stands as a symbol of bravery and national pride, surrounded by lush green lawns that are perfect for picnics and leisurely walks.

Designed by Sir Edwin Lutyens and inaugurated in 1931, India Gate has become one of Delhi's most visited landmarks. The structure is especially beautiful at night when illuminated, creating a mesmerizing view for tourists and locals alike.
