Dal Lake

About Dal Lake

Dal Lake is the heart of Srinagar, famous for its stunning mountain reflections, floating gardens, and the iconic Shikara rides. It is one of the most peaceful and photogenic places in India.

⭐ Best time to visit: March – June

Things to do

  • Shikara ride across the lake
  • Visit floating vegetable markets
  • Stay in the traditional Kashmiri houseboats
  • Try authentic Kahwa tea
