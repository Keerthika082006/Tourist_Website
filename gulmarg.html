Gulmarg — Snow Paradise

About Gulmarg

Gulmarg, known as the "Heart of Winter Sports in India", is a breathtaking hill station surrounded by snow-capped mountains, pine forests, and vast ski slopes. It is one of the top skiing destinations in Asia with the world-famous Gulmarg Gondola — one of the highest cable cars on Earth.

Whether it's snow activities, serene hikes, or stunning photography, Gulmarg offers a perfect blend of nature and adventure.
